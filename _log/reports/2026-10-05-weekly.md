**Grounding queries and Citation Share are not reachable through this API.** Verified
twice. 2026-09-22: five plausible method names probed, all 404, while GetQueryStats
returned 200. 2026-09-26: checked against Microsoft's own REST documentation, because
the deprecation notice made it worth re-checking. It does not change this. The endpoint
this script already uses (api.svc/json/) IS the migration target - only api.svc/soap/
and api.svc/pox/ are retired on 2026-08-31 - and Microsoft's documented method surface
is Rank and Traffic Stats, Link Details, Keyword Details, Crawl Stats, and URL, sitemap
and site submission. There is no AI Performance or grounding-query method in it.

closes it.** The AI Performance report is a manual look in Bing's own interface, or a
CSV exported by hand from it. Do not re-probe this; the answer is settled.
