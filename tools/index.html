Fuel Cost Calculator

Open the calculator›
Miles, MPG, and the price at the pump give what the trip costs, with the planning cushion already added. Any vehicle works: RV types use their real-world MPG range, and cars, SUVs, pickups and motorcycles use the Energy Department average for that kind of vehicle. Your own measured MPG wins over every preset.

$552 trip fuel, middle

Best case: $477 at 11 MPG · Worst case: $656 at 8 MPG

Your own figure: 10.0 MPG

An estimate for planning. The pump is the source of truth.

Watts to Amps & Shore Power

Open the calculator›
Convert watts and amps either way at 120, 240, or 12 volts, and check the appliances you run at the same time against what your 30-amp or 50-amp hookup actually supplies. The 50-amp cord is 12,000 watts, not 6,000, and the tool shows why.

12.5 A 1,500 W at 120 volts

Volts: 120 V · Watts: 1,500 W

30-amp service: 3,600 W

Typical figures from Cummins' own chart. Your appliance's label wins.

Solar Sizing Calculator

Open the calculator›
Daily watt-hours divided by peak sun hours, with NREL's 0.77 derate applied and the maker-recommended margin shown alongside. The region presets use real peak-sun-hour figures for the country, from about 3 in the Northwest to 6.5 in the desert Southwest.

623 W panels needed

Makers' formula: 2,400 Wh / 5.0 hr = 480 W · After the 0.77 derate: 623 W nameplate

480 W panels needed

A starting point for shopping. Winter sun and shade cut output below the rated hours.

RV Loan Calculator

Open the calculator›
The monthly payment and the total interest on an RV loan, from the price, down payment, rate, and term, by the standard amortization formula. The result shows what the loan really costs by the end, because a longer term spreads the interest out, hiding it inside a smaller payment.

$633.64 monthly payment

Borrowed: $32,000 at 7.00% · Total interest: $6,018 over 5 years

$371.55 monthly payment

Not a loan offer.

RV Weight & Towing Calculator

Open the calculator›
Towing capacity vs the 80% rule, door-sticker payload, tongue or pin load, trailer GVWR, per-axle estimates. Type once, everything recomputes, the answer to "can my truck actually tow this?" in three fields.

5,587 lb loaded trailer

Towing capacity: 51% of 11,000 lb · Truck payload: 1,070 / 2,200 lb

Trailer payload: 6,471 / 7,000 lb

Estimates only. Always confirm your real load at a certified scale.

RV Toilet Water Valve Finder

Find my valve›
The toilet water valve is what fails when water is left in the line over a freeze, and it is bought with no part number in hand, because the model label is usually gone. Answer what you can see about your Dometic or Thetford toilet and get the maker's number, with the document it came from.

385311641 water valve kit

Flush: Foot pedal · Maker: Dometic

Not for macerator or cassette toilets.

RV Recall Lookup

Check my unit›
A recall is filed against a range of VINs, not against a model year, and a motorhome's VIN belongs to the chassis rather than the coach. Put in a VIN and this reads the federal recall and complaint records, or search by year, make and model, and it hands you the maker's lookup where the maker runs one.

21V685000 recall campaign

Make: Jayco · Model: Jay Flight

The United States is live. Canada comes from a snapshot.

RV Water Heater Anode Rod Finder

Find my rod›
Many water heaters have no anode rod, and must not be given one: a Suburban tank is porcelain-lined steel with a sacrificial rod, and an Atwood or Dometic tank is aluminum with a drain plug, which Dometic's own manual says a rod would damage. Answer what you can see and get the part number, or the answer that there is nothing to buy.

233514 anode rod, magnesium

Brand: Suburban · Tank: Porcelain-lined steel

Covers tank heaters, not tankless.

RV Converter and Power Center Finder

Find my replacement›
The converter in an older RV is usually a model nobody sells any more, and every maker's cross-reference chart is written from its own side. Type the model on the unit and this reads both parsed charts and shows what each maker publishes as the replacement, cited to the chart it came from.

PD4060KV replaces a Parallax 7355

Maker: Progressive Dynamics · Fits: 30 amp wall mount

WFCO's wider chart is linked rather than read.

RV Appliance Control Board Finder

Find my board›
A fridge that stops cooling, a generator that will not start, a furnace that will not light: the board is a common cause, though check the simple things first. Dinosaur Electronics publishes the only public charts pairing a maker's model or part number with the board that replaces it, across four charts. Where a model was built with more than one board, every production range is shown rather than one being picked for you.

Micro P-711 replaces a Dometic board

Fits: RM 2652, by range · Norcold: control + eyebrow

Fridges, generators and ignitors. All four charts read.

RV Water Filter Finder

Find my filter›
A water filter either screws onto the hose or sits in a housing, and which one you can use turns on the thread at the inlet and the size of the cartridge coming out of the housing. Answer what you can see and this lands on the maker's figures for an inline filter or a cartridge format, or says that the maker does not publish the figure you asked for.

Slim Line 10 inch by 2.5 inch

End cap: Double open end · Big Blue: 4.5 inch, will not fit

2.5 inch is not 4.5 inch, and 10 is not 20.

Battery Runtime Calculator

Open the calculator›
How long the house batteries carry your gear before they need charging. The amp-hours on the label, the chemistry, and the watts you draw: lead-acid gives half its amp-hours, lithium most of them, and the tool applies the right fraction.

17.1 hr runtime estimate

Label says: 100 Ah = 1,280 Wh · Usable at 80%: 1,024 Wh

Usable at 50%: 1,200 Wh

Label figures and maker-published fractions. A battery monitor is the source of truth.

Tire Date Code Decoder

Open the decoder›
A tire made since 2000 carries the week and year it was made in the last four digits of its DOT code. Type those four digits and get the date and your tire's age, with the six-to-10-year replacement window NHTSA cites.

1024 date digits

Built in the: tenth week · Year: 2024

2321 date digits

Reads the date only. It cannot judge whether a tire is safe.

Roof Snow Load Calculator

Open the calculator›
Snow's weight is just the weight of the water in it. Enter how deep it is on the roof, or its water equivalent if you have one, and this gives you the load in pounds per square foot, and compares it to your roof's rating.

7.5 lb/ft² load carried

Against your rating: 25% of 30 lb/ft² · Snow on the roof: 6.0 inches

15.0 lb/ft² load carried

Not a structural assessment.

State Trailer Brake Laws

Look up my state›
Whether a trailer has to have brakes is set by the state you tow in, not by a national rule. The 3,000 pound figure everyone repeats is a federal exception written for commercial vehicles. Pick a state and this shows the threshold its own vehicle code states, quoted, with the section named and linked to the state's site.

3,000 lb the number everyone repeats

California: 1,500 lb · Idaho: 1,500 lb unladen

Every state read so far, each from its own code.
