Fuel Cost Calculator

Runs in your browser

Miles, MPG, and the price at the pump give what the trip costs, with the planning cushion already added. Any vehicle works: RV types use their real-world MPG range, and cars, SUVs, pickups and motorcycles use the Energy Department average for that kind of vehicle. Your own measured MPG wins over every preset.

Watts to Amps & Shore Power

Runs in your browser

Convert watts and amps either way at 120, 240, or 12 volts, and check the appliances you run at the same time against what your 30-amp or 50-amp hookup actually supplies. The 50-amp cord is 12,000 watts, not 6,000, and the tool shows why.

Runs in your browser

Roof Snow Load Calculator

Runs in your browser

Snow's weight is just the weight of the water in it. Enter how deep it is on the roof, or its water equivalent if you have one, and this gives you the load in pounds per square foot by the National Weather Service's own method, and compares it to your roof's rating.

Not a structural assessment.
