Roof Snow Load Calculator

Runs in your browser

Snow is water with air in it, so what it weighs is what the water in it weighs. Enter the water equivalent and your roof pitch and this gives you the load in pounds per square foot, by the National Weather Service's own method, and compares it with a rating if your maker publishes one.

Open the calculator

A rough estimate by the Weather Service's own description. It is not a structural assessment.

Water equivalent2.0 inches
Flat roof load10.4 lb/ft²
At 35° pitch8.5 lb/ft²

The Weather Service's own worked example.

RV Loan Calculator

Runs in your browser

The monthly payment and the total interest on an RV loan, from the price, down payment, rate, and term, by the standard amortization formula. The result shows what the loan really costs by the end, because a longer term hides the interest inside a smaller payment.

Not a loan offer. The rate a lender quotes you is the only one that counts.

Tire Date Code Decoder

Runs in your browser

Every tire carries the week and year it was made in the last four digits of its DOT code. Type those four digits and read the date and the age off the sidewall, with the rule quoted from NHTSA rather than paraphrased.

Open the decoder

Reads the date only. It cannot judge whether a tire is safe.

Watts to Amps & Shore Power

Runs in your browser

Convert watts and amps either way at 120, 240, or 12 volts, and check the appliances you run at the same time against what your 30-amp or 50-amp hook-up actually supplies. The 50-amp cord is 12,000 watts, not 6,000, and the tool shows why.

Open the calculator

Typical figures from Cummins' own chart. Your appliance's label wins.

Fuel Cost Calculator

Runs in your browser

Miles, MPG, and the price at the pump give what the trip costs, with the planning cushion already added. Any vehicle works: RV types are priced across their real-world MPG range, and cars, SUVs, pickups and motorcycles use the Energy Department average for that kind of vehicle. Your own measured MPG wins over every preset.

Open the calculator

An estimate for planning. The pump is the source of truth.

Worst case$656 at 8 MPG

Example: 1,200 miles in a Class C at $4.37.

Solar Sizing Calculator
