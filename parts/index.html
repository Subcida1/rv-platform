Every part of an RV

An RV is 157 parts across 9 systems. Each part: what it does, the names people use for it, which RVs have one, and where to go next. 62 of them already have a repair guide here, 83 link to a guide or the maker's own documentation, and the other 74 are here so you can put a name to the thing that broke.

Towing and running gear

Everything between the tow vehicle and the road, which is where a breakdown strands you.

22 parts

  • Hitch coupler

    Also called coupler, ball coupler, A-frame coupler

    Clamps the trailer tongue onto the hitch ball.

  • Tongue jack

    Also called trailer jack, A-frame jack, electric tongue jack, power jack

    Raises and lowers the tongue so the trailer can be hitched and levelled.

    Barker manuals

  • Stabilizer jacks

    Also called scissor jacks, stabilisers, stabilizing jacks

    Hold the trailer steady once it is parked, they do not lift it.

    How to fix it

  • Leveling jacks (hydraulic)

    Also called hydraulic levelers, HWH jacks, level-up system, jacks down light

    Lift and level a motorhome on its own suspension.

    How to fix it

  • Weight-distribution hitch

    Also called WD hitch, equalizer hitch, load leveling hitch

    Moves tongue weight forward onto the tow vehicle's front axle.

  • Sway control

    Also called sway bar, anti-sway, friction sway control

    Resists trailer sway from wind and passing trucks.

    Hensley manuals

  • Safety chains

    Also called breakaway chains

    Catch the trailer if the coupler fails.

  • Breakaway switch

    Also called breakaway cable, breakaway kit

    Applies the trailer brakes if it separates from the tow vehicle.

  • Trailer brakes

    Also called electric brakes, surge brakes, brake assembly

    Slow and stop the trailer itself.

    How to fix it

  • Brake controller

    Also called trailer brake controller, proportional controller

    Tells the trailer brakes how hard to apply.

    How to fix it

  • Axle

    Also called trailer axle, torsion axle, spring axle

    Carries the weight and holds the wheels.

    Dexter manuals

  • Leaf springs and shackles

    Also called suspension, equalizer, shackle kit, wet bolt kit

    Spring the trailer over bumps.

  • Wheel bearings

    Also called bearings, races, grease seals, bearing buddy

    Let the wheel spin freely on the spindle.

    How to fix it

  • Tires

    Also called ST tires, load range, speed rating

    Carry the load and absorb the road.

    Carlstar manuals

  • Tire pressure monitoring

    Also called TPMS, tire sensors, pressure monitor

    Warns before a tire fails.

    EEZ RV Products manuals TST Truck System Technologies manuals

  • Lug nuts and torque

    Also called wheel torque, lug pattern

    Hold the wheel on.

  • Fifth-wheel landing gear

    Also called front jacks, landing legs, electric landing gear

    Raise and lower the front of a fifth wheel to hitch it.

  • Kingpin and fifth-wheel hitch

    Also called kingpin box, fifth wheel hitch, slider hitch

    Connects a fifth wheel to the truck bed.

    Reese manuals

  • Toad setup

    Also called dinghy tow, tow bar, baseplate, flat tow

    Lets a motorhome pull a car with four wheels down.

    Demco manuals Roadmaster manuals

  • Air suspension and helper springs

    Also called air bags, sumo springs, helper springs

    Carry heavy loads without squatting.

  • Wheel chocks and jack pads

    Also called chocks, leveling blocks, jack pad, stabilizer pad

    Stops the trailer rolling and keeps a jack off soft ground.

  • Tire chains

    Also called snow chains, cable chains, traction devices

    Gets you over a pass in a chain-control area.

    How to fix it

Power and electrical

Two systems in one place: the 120 volt side from the pedestal, and the 12 volt side that runs the coach.

23 parts

  • Shore power cord

    Also called power cord, shore cord, 30 amp cord, 50 amp cord

    Brings park power into the coach.

  • Shore power inlet

    Also called power inlet, twist lock inlet, receptacle

    Where the cord meets the coach.

  • Surge protector and EMS

    Also called EMS, surge guard, electrical management

    Shields the coach from bad park power.

  • Transfer switch

    Also called ATS, automatic transfer switch

    Chooses between shore power and generator.

  • Breaker panel and 120V breakers

    Also called distribution panel, main breaker, GFCI breaker

    Protects the circuits inside the coach.

  • Converter and charger

    Also called converter, battery charger, power center, WFCO, Progressive Dynamics

    Turns 120 volts into 12 volts and charges the house battery.

    How to fix it Go Power! manuals Progressive Dynamics manuals WFCO manuals

  • Inverter

    Also called inverter, pure sine, modified sine

    Makes 120 volts from the batteries for off grid use.

    Go Power! manuals Magnum Energy manuals Progressive Dynamics manuals Renogy manuals Rich Solar manuals Samlex America manuals Victron Energy manuals Xantrex manuals

  • Inverter charger

    Also called inverter/charger, Xantrex, Magnum

    Combines inverting and charging in one unit.

    Magnum Energy manuals Xantrex manuals

  • House battery

    Also called coach battery, deep cycle, AGM, LiFePO4, lithium

    Stores power for everything that runs without shore power.

    How to fix it Battle Born manuals Renogy manuals

  • Battery disconnect

    Also called kill switch, battery switch, isolator switch

    Cuts the house battery from the coach.

    How to fix it

  • Battery monitor and shunt

    Also called shunt, battery monitor, state of charge

    Tells you what is really left in the bank.

  • Solar panels

    Also called solar, PV panels

    Charge the batteries from the sun.

    How to fix it EcoFlow manuals Go Power! manuals Jackery manuals Renogy manuals Rich Solar manuals Zamp Solar manuals

  • Solar charge controller

    Also called MPPT, PWM controller, solar controller

    Manages what the panels send to the batteries.

    How to fix it Magnum Energy manuals Victron Energy manuals Zamp Solar manuals

  • Fuses

    Also called blade fuse, ANL fuse, class T, 12 volt fuse, inline fuse

    Protect every 12 volt circuit.

    How to fix it

  • 12 volt outlets and USB

    Also called cigarette outlet, USB port, 12v socket

    Power small devices from the coach battery.

  • 120 volt outlets and GFCI

    Also called receptacles, GFCI outlet, outlet not working

    Run household appliances on shore or generator power.

    How to fix it

  • Light fixtures and LEDs

    Also called 12 volt lights, LED lights, dome lights, porch light

    Light the inside and outside.

    How to fix it

  • Wiring harness and connectors

    Also called 12 volt wiring, wire nuts, scotchloks, junction box

    Carry power and signals through the coach.

  • Generator

    Also called onan, genset, built in generator

    Makes its own power when there is no pedestal.

    How to fix it Champion manuals Cummins Onan manuals Firman manuals Generac manuals Honda manuals WEN manuals Westinghouse manuals

  • Control and monitor panel

    Also called monitor panel, control panel, touch panel

    Shows tank levels, battery, and runs systems.

    How to fix it

  • Portable generator

    Also called inverter generator, Honda eu2200, Firman, Westinghouse

    Power away from a pedestal without running the coach generator.

  • Battery combiner and ACR

    Also called ACR, automatic charging relay, battery separator, combiner

    Lets the alternator charge both banks without linking them when parked.

  • Cell and wifi booster

    Also called cell booster, weboost, wifi booster, antenna

    Pulls a usable signal where there is barely one.

Water and plumbing

18 parts

  • Fresh water tank

    Also called potable tank, holding tank fresh

    Stores drinking and washing water on board.

    How to fix it

  • Gravity fill and city water inlet

    Also called gravity fill, city water connection, fresh water inlet

    Two ways to put water in the tank or run off park pressure.

  • Water pressure regulator

    Also called pressure regulator, inline regulator

    Protects the plumbing from park pressure that is too high.

  • Inline water filter

    Also called water filter, sediment filter, carbon filter

    Takes sediment and taste out of park water.

  • Water pump

    Also called 12 volt pump, demand pump, Shurflo, Remco

    Pressurises the system from the fresh tank.

    How to fix it Valterra manuals

  • Pump strainer and check valve

    Also called strainer, inlet filter, check valve

    Keeps debris out of the pump and stops backflow.

    How to fix it

  • Accumulator tank

    Also called pressure tank, accumulator

    Smooths the pump's cycling.

  • PEX lines and fittings

    Also called pex, sharkbite, crimp rings, water lines

    Carry water through the coach.

    How to fix it

  • Low point drains

    Also called drain valves, low point drain

    Empty the lines for winter.

    How to fix it

  • Water heater (tank)

    Also called Atwood, Suburban, 6 gallon, 10 gallon

    Heats water for taps and shower.

    How to fix it Eemax manuals Lippert manuals Suburban manuals Truma manuals

  • Water heater (tankless)

    Also called on demand, Truma AquaGo, Girard, Fogatti

    Heats water as it flows.

    How to fix it Eemax manuals Lippert manuals Suburban manuals Truma manuals

  • Water heater anode rod

    Also called anode, sacrificial anode

    Sacrifices itself to protect the tank.

    How to fix it

  • Water heater bypass

    Also called bypass kit, winterizing bypass, three way valve

    Lets you winterise without filling the heater.

    How to fix it

  • Faucets and shower mixer

    Also called kitchen faucet, bath faucet, shower valve

    Deliver hot and cold water.

  • Shower pan and drain

    Also called shower drain, shower pan, grey tank drain

    Drains the shower to the grey tank.

  • Outside shower

    Also called exterior shower, utility shower

    Rinse gear and pets outside.

    How to fix it

  • Water system winterising

    Also called antifreeze, blow out, winterize

    Protects the plumbing from freezing.

    How to fix it

  • Water softener and filtration

    Also called water softener, onboard filter, portable softener

    Takes hardness and taste out of park water.

  • Tank flush and rinsing

  • Vent stack and air admittance valve

    How to fix it

  • P-traps and drains

    Also called p trap, sink drain, shower trap

    Keeps sewer gas out of the living space.
