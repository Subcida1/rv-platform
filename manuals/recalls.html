RV RECALLS AND SERVICE BULLETINS

How to find out whether your RV, or something fitted to it, has been recalled, and where the manufacturer service bulletins are kept.

Check your own unit

Start with the federal lookup. It takes a VIN, or a year, a make and a model, and returns the recalls, complaints and investigations filed against that unit. Two makers run their own lookup as well.

  • NHTSA recall lookup keyed by VIN, or year and make and model
    Search recalls for a specific unit. Enter a VIN, or browse by year, make and model, and see the complaints and investigations filed against it. Free, no account.
    link checked 2026-09-21
  • Grand Design owner recalls keyed by campaign
    The maker's own recall hub. Grand Design is the one brand observed publishing a letter per campaign, in both the US and Canadian versions.
    link checked 2026-09-21
  • Forest River VIN recall search keyed by 17-digit VIN
    The maker's own recall lookup by VIN.

Recalls you can read in full

The campaign number is the thread through a recall: the federal report, the maker's own notice and any repair instruction all carry it.

  • Forest River recall report, campaign 24V047 keyed by campaign number
    The federal report for a furnace that could overheat and crack its burn chamber, covering certain 2023 and 2024 Prism Class C motorhomes.

  • Grand Design recall letter, campaign 26V-446 keyed by campaign number

Service bulletins

A recall is a safety defect. A service bulletin is the maker telling its dealers how to deal with something, and the federal government collects them alongside the recalls. Almost nobody in the RV world links them.

  • NHTSA manufacturer communications, 2025 to 2026 keyed by make, model, model year and component
    Every manufacturer communication filed in the window, including the RV makers, as one federal file. 2,063 of its 769,391 records are from RV manufacturers.
    we could not check this link automatically
  • NHTSA manufacturer communications, field layout keyed by field layout
    What each column in the file above means, so a bulletin can be matched to a make and a model year before it is read.
    link checked 2026-09-21
  • Grand Design service bulletin, slide water intrusion keyed by document id
    One bulletin out of that file, as an example of what the record holds: the slide may not fully retract at the bottom on 25FW Lineage models built between 6 May 2024 and 25 July 2025.
    link checked 2026-09-21

Appliances and components

The fridge, the water heater, the generator and the portable heater are consumer products before they are RV parts, so they are recalled through a different agency.

  • US Consumer Product Safety Commission keyed by product
    Recalls for the appliances that go into an RV. A fridge, a water heater, a generator and a portable heater are consumer products first, and CPSC covers them separately from the vehicle.
    link checked 2026-09-21

Canada

Why the bulletin file is worth knowing about

The federal system that holds recalls also holds every manufacturer communication, which is where a maker explains a problem to its dealers before it becomes a recall, or when it never does. The RV makers file them in the thousands, and nothing on the consumer side indexes them by make and model.

Reading one takes two steps: find the document id in the file, then open the bulletin itself at a link built from that id. The ids are not guessable, so start from the file rather than from a pattern.
