Built from inside an RV

Ty has lived full-time in a 33-foot travel trailer since 2020.

Where this comes from

Six years of full-time RV living teaches the questions nobody makes guides for: how do I get my RV steps up? how do I stop my hose from freezing? how do you find the right RV plumbing valve that isn't standard? where do I find these parts? which campgrounds are worthwhile? how are the various ways you can tow a car with a motorhome? how much weight can my trailer and vehicle take?

Every tool on OriginRV was made because it was a real question or issue, and we provide all these tools to you for free.

The weight calculator is a tool I used myself to figure out just how the weight was situated in my setup, and how to optimize my towing strategies. Every guide carries the date its claims were last checked. The directory lists each business, what regions they serve, and what services they provide. From mobile technicians to traditional shops and 24x7 towing help, we have you covered.

Our parts reference names every RV component by system and says what each one does, breaking down the entire RV into a search index, allowing users to easily locate, identify and find information on nearly any RV part you can imagine, and the manuals library links each maker's documentation directly.

How information is sourced and verified

Each guide names the documents behind its claims and links them at the foot of the page: the maker's manual, the state statute, the recall notice, the federal agency publication. Where two sources disagree, both are named. Where a figure is a maker's specification rather than a standard, the page says which it is. Where nothing authoritative exists, it says that too.

The date at the top of a guide is the date its claims were last checked against those documents, not the date it was written. Any reasoning that is ours rather than a source's is labeled as such.

Liability and safety

Working on an RV involves complex mechanical, electrical, and gas systems. Improper handling of electricity, propane, towing loads, and vehicle weight can result in property damage, injury, or loss.

The guides on this site are intended to be informative, cited to the documents behind them. However, we cannot independently verify every procedure or scenario against every possible custom setup, and we do not guarantee the absolute completeness or error-free accuracy of all content. Follow your RV's owner's manual wherever it differs from anything you find online. When a step is beyond what you are confident doing, stop and have it done professionally.

We are not liable for damage, injury, or loss that follows from using this site. You work on your own RV at your own risk.

The calculators here return estimates. Verify all numbers against real world weights to ensure accurate and safe towing. Always check and cross-reference your vehicle's certification label or the book that came with it. The directory lists businesses from what each publishes publicly; it does not license, insure, or inspect them. Confirm a business's credentials and coverages with the business before you hire it.
