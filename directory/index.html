Where these listings are

Coverage follows where the businesses are. The Pacific coast and the I-5 corridor are the most complete, and the desert and mountain corridors have the fewest listings because they have the fewest businesses. Where a stretch is empty, the state pages say so rather than listing somebody who is not there.

What a listing carries

Every entry names the business, the area it covers, the phone number, and what the shop or mobile technician actually does. Listings are free.
