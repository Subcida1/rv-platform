What this site collects, and what it does not

OriginRV tracks visits and reads the words people type into its search box. It does not have accounts, does not run advertising, and does not sell personal information. This page describes what is collected.

What is collected

Visits, through Google Analytics. OriginRV loads Google Analytics, which sets cookies and records things like which pages were opened and roughly where the visit came from, what kind of device and browser was used, and which site linked here. Google processes that data as described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visit counts, through Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare records page views for this site without setting a cookie and without building a profile of you.

The words you type into the site search. The search box on this site sends the term you typed to Google Analytics, so we can see what people are looking for and what is missing. It is recorded with the visit rather than with any account, and it is not a record of who searched.

What you send us. If you use the claim form or email us, we receive what you wrote, including your email address if you gave one. That is used to answer you and to correct the listing, and for nothing else.

What is not collected

There are no accounts and no logins on this site. Our own code sets no cookies; Google Analytics does, as described above. No advertising network runs here, no data is sold, and no profile of your browsing is built across other websites.

Cookies

Cookies come from Google Analytics. A browser set to block third-party cookies, or an extension that blocks analytics, won't break the site. Every tool and every guide works even if you refuse all cookies.

One page carries a video. The towing guide shows a still frame from YouTube, and the player itself is only built when you press play, so no YouTube player, scripts, or cookies load unless you choose to watch. Pressing play hands that visit to YouTube, on Google's terms, like any embedded video.

Do Not Track

This site does not change what it collects when a browser sends a Do Not Track signal, because it does not track anyone across other websites in the first place. The setting doesn't change anything here.

Businesses listed in the directory

The directory of RV repair businesses is built from public information each business publishes: their own websites, their own contact details. It is a compilation of public information, not material supplied by the businesses, and it is provided as is: a listing being present is not a recommendation, and a business's details can change after they're recorded.

If you run a listed business and want the entry corrected, updated or removed, just say so and we'll take care of it. No reason needed.

Asking about your own data

Write to the address on the contact page and ask what information we have about you, or ask for it to be deleted. Because there is no account system, the personal data we hold is what you send us: a message and our reply, or a claim-form request (the business name, city, state, phone and website you enter). Requests are answered, and what we hold is deleted when you ask.

This policy describes what the site actually does; it isn't a template. If what it does changes, this page changes with it.
