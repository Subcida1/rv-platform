Why we built it

Dry weight is a museum number

What matters is what the RV weighs loaded: water in the tanks, propane, gear, passengers. This calculator does that math and shows you the verdict in color.

⚖️ The 80% rule

Max tow ratings assume flat road, no wind, sea level. Real roads have hills, passes, and panic stops. Keeping loaded weight at or under 80% of rated capacity is the buffer experienced RVers respect.
